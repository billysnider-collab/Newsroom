Polymarket

Specialty desk. Prediction-market prices as facts about markets — not trading advice, not a forecast of what “should” happen.

Terms (first use): Polymarket = a public prediction market where people buy Yes/No shares on whether a stated event happens. Absolute majority = more than half the seats (here, more than 41.5 of 83 — so 42+). Floored price = Yes shares trading near zero after the result is clear — mostly leftover settlement noise, not a live forecast. Soft Publish = site story only when a primary confirms a change.

Soft Publish #1 · Not a homepage lead

Sachsen-Anhalt: AfD won seats — not an absolute majority

In plain English

In Germany’s Sachsen-Anhalt election, AfD won 39 of 83 seats. That’s a strong showing — but not an absolute majority (you need 42+). On Polymarket, the “absolute majority — Yes” contract is floored around ~$0.0015. That tiny price is leftover settlement after the result, not a prediction that the majority might still happen.

What we know

  • AfD: 39 / 83 seats — no absolute majority
  • Polymarket absolute-majority YES floored (~$0.0015) = post-result resolution carry

What we don’t (HOLD)

  • UMA resolution details on the exact conditionId — HOLD until primary confirms

Why it matters

Don’t confuse a wiped-out Yes price with a live forecast. After the count, the market is mostly cleaning up — the news is the seat math.

No trading advice. This desk reports what the market priced and what the result was — not what you should buy or sell.
