How to read this
Mid / midpoint
Halfway between best bid and best ask — more robust than last trade.
Spread
Ask minus bid; the honest error bar. Wide spread = noisy “probability.”
Priced delta
How much the mid moved over a window — we flag big ones.
Liquidity / depth
Dollars sitting near the mid; thin books can be moved by one trader.
Resolution language
The exact Yes/No rules; misreads cause fake “mispricing.”
UMA / oracle
Polymarket International doesn’t decide outcomes; proposals/disputes can leave markets open after the event.
HOLD
We don’t know causation, or the number shouldn’t be treated as a forecast.
Edge (newsroom sense)
Interesting market structure or signal-vs-evidence gap — not a bet tip.
Sidebar not homepage
Market signal supports the dossier; spicy mids aren’t national leads alone.
