Coming to terms — tap to define jargon

Season + DFS vocabulary — so we’re talking about the same things.

Season fantasy vs DFS
Season = draft/start-sit for your league week. DFS = one-day lineups with a salary cap. Same news can matter for both; GPP angles care more about ownership and uniqueness.
GPP
Guaranteed Prize Pool tournament — big field, top-heavy payouts. You often want lower-owned players who can go off, not just the safest chalk.
Showdown
A DFS contest built on a single game (here: Wed NE@SEA). Fewer players, different captain/FLEX rules than the main slate.
Chalk
The popular, high-ownership play everyone expects. Fine in cash games; in GPP, too much chalk can tie you with the field.
Ownership / leverage
Ownership = how many lineups start a player. Leverage = fading or pivoting when the public piles onto chalk — if you’re right, you jump the field.
Ceiling / floor
Ceiling = boom week potential. Floor = what you get if things go poorly. GPP leans ceiling; season start-sit balances both.
DNP
Did Not Practice — a practice report tag. A long DNP streak before Week 1 is a strong sit/OUT lean until designation flips.
PUP
Physically Unable to Perform list — player misses at least the first four games. Opens a volume vacuum (see Charbonnet → Price).
ADP
Average Draft Position — where the industry is drafting someone. Compare ADP to expected role to spot under/overpriced players.
FAAB
Free Agent Acquisition Budget — the dollars you bid to claim waivers. Escalate only when practice reports confirm the contingency.
Commissioner’s Exempt
League list that keeps a player off the field while a legal/administrative issue plays out (Jacobs) — creates temporary committee chaos.
