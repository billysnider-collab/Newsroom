Black Sea · the war widens — EVENING

A drone sinks a ship in Bulgaria's waters — crew missing

Two ships were hit Tuesday in a combined air-and-sea drone attack about 70 nautical miles off Byala, in Bulgaria's exclusive economic zone — the first such attack in Bulgarian waters (Reuters). A Togo-flagged merchant ship sank — its crew is missing; a grain ship's crew was rescued with two injured. PM Radev called it 'a gross violation of international law' but said it's unclear who launched the drones; Zelenskyy says a joint probe points to Russia; Moscow has made no comment. Saudi Arabia — EVENING

Two airports struck; the coalition retakes ground

Saudi Arabia's civil aviation authority said Tuesday that Monday-evening attacks damaged Jazan's King Abdullah bin Abdulaziz International Airport and Najran International Airport — three minor injuries, material damage (Reuters, WSJ, AP). The statement did NOT name the attacker; the Houthis claimed the strikes and warned airlines to avoid Saudi airspace except over Mecca and Medina. On the ground, Saudi-backed Yemeni forces retook Dhubab, Bab and Hadeid around Bab el-Mandeb (AP). Riyadh-airport and Rabigh-refinery claims remain unverified.

Hormuz · the tanker war — EVENING

12 injured on the On Peace — the first blood

The Panama-flagged tanker On Peace was struck by an unknown projectile in the Strait of Hormuz Monday — engine-room fire; 12 crew injured, 11 of them Indian nationals, evacuated with Omani assistance to Khasab, Oman (Reuters, AFP, India's foreign ministry). India condemned the attack. The Greek LNG carrier Maran Gas Mystras was also hit late Monday — crew OK (TradeWinds/Ambrey). UKMTO logged five incidents Monday.
