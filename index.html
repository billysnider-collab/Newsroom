UNGA · Tehran answers

Pezeshkian answered: defiance and an open door

Tehran picked its track. Pezeshkian told UNGA Wednesday that Iran is “ready for dialogue and diplomacy and negotiations without accepting the language of force” — and “will never bow our heads or bend at the knee.” He held up photos of the late Supreme Leader Khamenei and the schoolchildren killed in the Feb-28 strikes, refused to surrender Iran’s nuclear rights (“no” to nuclear weapons, “no” to restrictions on nuclear technology), and warned: “we either all live in security, or we will have to live in insecurity together.” The sole American delegate in the hall walked out. (WSJ, AP, NY Post, AnewZ)

Greenland · signed

Greenland signed — now read the fine print

The trilateral signing is done (Trump, Frederiksen, Nielsen): two new US “Defense Areas” (Narsarsuaq, Mestersvig) plus an expanded Pituffik, non-NATO exclusion, Golden Dome allowed, no end date. But signed is not in effect — it takes effect only after Danish and Greenlandic parliaments complete their procedures, and the new sites’ scope has a 90-day clock with ministerial escalation. “Permanent” is a press word; the parliaments are the mechanism.
