Markets · ISM — MIDDAY

ISM 54.9 — the prices print the hawks wanted

ISM Services for September came in at 54.9 vs 55.4 in August (forecast ~55.1) — the 27th straight month of expansion, but cooling. The prices-paid index jumped to 74.0, the highest since July 2022; employment nudged back to expansion at 50.1; new orders eased to 59.8. Hormuz — MIDDAY

The IRGC ordered a tanker to turn back

A tanker transiting Hormuz was hailed by the IRGC and ordered to turn back or risk being targeted — the master complied, 11 nautical miles north of Khasab, Oman (UKMTO, via Reuters). The coercion is now direct, not just kinetic: seven strikes since Sept 28 plus a turn-back order on the same waterway. A fresh WSJ piece notes early signs oil flows are beginning to slow, with firm data lagging as ships run dark. The Bab el-Mandeb incident (explosions ~60nm south of Mokha) is still unconfirmed as connected.
