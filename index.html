Isaias — MIDDAY

NHC: it stays a major hurricane at landfall

The 11am ET NHC advisory keeps Isaias at 120 mph — winds “may have leveled off,” with shear aligned with the storm's motion instead of weakening it — and expects it to remain a major hurricane at landfall this evening near Pensacola. First Cat 3 landfall for Pensacola since Dennis 2005 (per NHC, Pensacola News Journal). Shut-ins hold at ~1.3M b/d — 62.9% of Gulf output.
