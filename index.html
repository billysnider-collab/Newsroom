US · sanctions

Graham signed — the 100% tariff weapon goes live, five days before Xi arrives

Signed Friday: H.R. 5334, the Lindsey O. Graham Sanctioning Russia and Iran Act of 2026 (White House statement, per Reuters). It authorizes up to 100% tariffs on major buyers of Russian oil (China, India), mandates blocking sanctions within 30 days, and extends Iran sanctions. The House passed it 262-159 (Sept 16); the Senate 86-11 (August). The authority is entirely discretionary — Trump decides whether and whom to hit. No tariff designations or implementation timeline yet.
