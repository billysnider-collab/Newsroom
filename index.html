US · sanctions

Trump signs the Graham act — Russian energy, shadow fleet, up to 100% tariffs

Signed Friday: H.R. 5334, the Lindsey O. Graham Sanctioning Russia and Iran Act of 2026 (White House statement + Reuters). It targets Russian energy, defense, and the shadow fleet, authorizes up to 100% tariffs on the top five importers of Russian oil and gas — India in the crosshairs — with a 5-year sunset, and prevents a lapse in Iran sanctions authority. The House passed it 262-159 on Wednesday; Trump sat on it for two days after a White House official told WSJ he planned to sign. No tariff designations or implementation timeline yet.

Hormuz · the workaround

The Sohar flow hardens — record freight, Japan covered through November

Reuters, Friday: the ~60M barrels of Saudi crude committed from Ras Tanura for Sept/Oct loading via ship-to-ship transfer off Oman’s Sohar is now a confirmed trade flow — Chinese and South Korean refiners the primary buyers, India and Japan also in. VLCC freight out of Fujairah for early-October loadings hit a record 800 Worldscale. The Petroleum Association of Japan says Japanese refineries have crude secured through November.
