Russia · refineries

Perm refinery halted — Russia’s 7th-largest plant down after Friday drone strikes

Reuters, Friday: Russia’s Perm refinery (LUKOIL Permnefteorgsintez — 252,000 bpd, seventh-largest in Russia) halted operations after Friday drone strikes, per two industry sources; fire damaged pipelines, storage, and units. The same overnight wave damaged the Novoshakhtinsk refinery (Rostov), forcing a temporary suspension, per the governor. Russia’s MOD claims 592 drones were destroyed overnight; Lukoil declined to comment. Ukraine has not officially claimed this specific strike — but the Perm plant has now been hit five times since April.
