UNGA · the talks

Three hours in the room — and another meeting scheduled

US representatives (Kushner and Witkoff) held three hours of talks with Iranian officials on the UN sidelines Tuesday — Trump called them "very productive" with another meeting scheduled soon. "I think that a settlement is going to be reached." It is the first known face-to-face US–Iran contact since July’s collapsed interim deal. The afternoon caveat: Iran’s Fars News said sources denied the Kyodo-carried version of the Hormuz offer — the exact terms stay foggy. (NYPost, Reuters, Newsmax, WSJ)

Gulf leaders · the read-out

Trump: deal "right after" the midterms — or the Pickaxe strike

At the Gulf leaders meeting, Trump told the room an Iran deal could come "right after the midterm elections, maybe before" — then threatened to strike Pickaxe Mountain, a suspected Iranian nuclear site: "we’ll blow it up immediately" if the US sees activity. He claimed the Navy escorted "more than 1 billion barrels of oil" out of Hormuz and that a deal would send oil below prewar levels. The GCC bloc came in urging de-escalation. (NYPost, CNN)

Russia-Ukraine · wave one

The retaliation landed — and Zelensky answered with a truce offer

Russia’s first retaliation wave hit overnight: industrial sites in Dnipro, Kryvyi Rih, Pavlohrad and Poltava — 3 killed, 6 injured — with Oniks and ballistic missiles, four cruise missiles and 212 drones; ~100,000 consumers in Chernihiv lost power. Hours later, Zelensky asked Trump for a "winter package" of Patriot missiles and offered a mutual energy truce, saying he is ready for a trilateral with Putin "any time."
