Greenland · signed

The signatures are in — the text still isn’t

Trump, Frederiksen, and Nielsen signed the Greenland security agreement Tuesday morning in New York. Trump: "We’re going to have a tremendous relationship, a secure relationship... This was long in the making." Nielsen: "We are, have been, and will remain a steadfast friend of the United States." Frederiksen called it "a milestone in our longstanding cooperation." New detail (Fox News, White House official): Washington can establish additional installations without separate Danish or Greenlandic approval; adversaries barred from a military presence; a mechanism to block adversarial investment in sensitive sectors. The full text remains unpublished as of the signing; Copenhagen and Nuuk ratification is next. (USA Today, Fox News)
