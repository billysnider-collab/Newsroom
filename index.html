Africa · the new front

Tigray declares "full-blown war" — fighting spreads to Afar and Amhara

The TPLF declared the conflict a "full-blown war" on September 24, shattering the 2022 Pretoria peace deal that ended a war estimated to have killed ~600,000 people. Three airports seized; Ethiopian Airlines suspended Tigray flights; heavy artillery in Afar, heavy-weapons fire at Lalibela in Amhara. The federal army claims 272 fighters killed — unverified. NetBlocks confirmed an internet disruption Friday. The TPLF has allied with six other armed groups aiming to overthrow Abiy’s government.
