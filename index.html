Africa · Tigray

"A full war" — three airports seized, the grid blacked out

The Sept 23 offensive by the seven-group Ethiopian Peoples’ Forces Alliance for Survival widened across Tigray, Afar and Amhara: three airports seized (Aksum, Mekelle, Shire), the town of Abala captured, and a TDF official called it "full war" — shattering the 2022 Pretoria deal. The ENDF claims 272 fighters killed — unverified. NetBlocks confirmed a northern internet disruption; Reuters reports the Tigray side ordered it to blunt government drones.
