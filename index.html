First confirmed blood: 12 injured on the On Peace

Saudi Arabia — MIDDAY

Jazan and Najran airports confirmed struck

Saudi Arabia's civil aviation authority said Tuesday that Monday-evening attacks damaged Jazan's King Abdullah bin Abdulaziz International Airport and Najran International Airport — three minor injuries, material damage (Reuters, WSJ, AP). The statement did NOT name the attacker. The Houthis claimed the strikes late Monday and again warned international airlines to halt Saudi-airspace flights, except over Mecca and Medina.
