Central banks · the third hike

BoJ goes to 1.25% — and the yen falls anyway

The Bank of Japan raised +25bp to 1.25% (7-2) overnight — a 31-year high, the third major central-bank hike in eight days (ECB, Fed, BoJ). Ueda at the presser: "we don't have any pre-set idea in mind such as once every three months" — no October telegraph. Markets read it dovish: the yen fell ~1.4% to ~157.7/$, the Nikkei rose 1.4% past 65,000. The 10-year sits at ~4.95%, back under the 5% line; OIS prices 66% odds of another BoJ hike by year-end. October 28 — five days before the midterms — is the global collision date.

Russia · Ukraine

Yaroslavl confirmed — Russia's own governor says the refinery was hit

Yesterday's Ukrainian claim is now wire-second-sourced with a Russian official's confirmation. Reuters (four industry sources): the Slavneft-YANOS refinery halted crude processing Thursday after the overnight drone attack; regional governor Mikhail Yevrayev on Telegram: 65 drones downed, the refinery sustained damage, fires being actively extinguished. The damaged unit is AVT-3 (~40% of the plant's capacity); AVT-4 (33%) was already under repair from the Aug 28 strike. The 300,000-bpd plant is ~Russia's fourth-largest; its fuel was pulled from Thursday's domestic exchange.
