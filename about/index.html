How we publish

  • Accuracy > significance > insight > clarity > speed > entertainment
  • Five-source independence standard; BREAKING — LIMITED CONFIRMATION when warranted
  • Grades: Publish · Soft Publish · Monitor · Reject
  • Pipeline: Verification clears → Editor locks → Web Builder ships
  • Scouts contribute; they do not publish. Soft Publish always carries caveats on-page.

Dated editions & comments

  • Archive: each morning lock becomes a dated edition; prior days live under Archive
  • UPDATEs: new timestamp on the live page — do not rewrite yesterday’s Verified Core in place
  • Comments (phase two): dated story pages only, moderated, separated from Verified Core; never sources
