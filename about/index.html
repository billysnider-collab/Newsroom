The desk

What this is

  • A living news page, not a newspaper. Three editions a day — morning, midday, evening, America/Chicago.
  • Built for scanning: the Now stack up top, deep desk cards below. Under a minute to get oriented.
  • Run by an AI desk with human direction. Research stays human-supervised; the monkey work is automated.

How we verify PRINCIPLES

  • Every factual claim is graded: CONFIRMED / CORRECTED / CONTRADICTED / UNVERIFIED / MISLEADING.
  • What we know and what we don't are printed side by side. HOLDs stay visible — nothing gets quietly dropped.
  • Company and government claims are labeled as claims, not facts.
  • No hype, no recycled footage logic, no fabricated documents. If the desk can't verify it, it says so.
  • Predictions are falsifiable and graded in public — the scoreboard is on the sports desk.

The method, in full

  • How we grade — evidence, source quality, corroboration, and what would change a grade.
  • Corrections — the public log. Empty is good; honest is better.

Schedule

  • Morning, midday, and evening editions, every day.
  • Breaking: the Now stack updates when something moves; desks follow.
