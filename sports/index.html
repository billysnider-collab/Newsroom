Alvin Kamara (MCL) is officially questionable after full practice Friday, but beat reporter Nick Underhill says he won't have a "huge role" if active. Kamara is a bench in Week 1, flex only in deep leagues.
  • New England: TreVeyon Henderson (ankle) inactive for the opener — Rhamondre Stevenson handled 23 touches (18 carries for 51 yards, 5 catches for 44 yards). Henderson gets extra recovery with the next game Sept 20 vs Pittsburgh.

If he plays, he's the workhorse — and rookie Mike Washington Jr. (7.3 yards per carry this preseason) is a legit change-of-pace either way.
  • LA Chargers: Omarion Hampton leads, but OC Mike McDaniel warned outright he plans to use all three backs (Hampton, Keaton Mitchell, Kimani Vidal) "probably every game" on a hot-hand basis. Mitchell has standalone flex value.
  • Green Bay: OC Adam Stenavich declared a committee with Josh Jacobs on the Commissioner's Exempt List — MarShawn Lloyd starts and should lead early downs (12–15 touches); Chris Brooks handles passing downs. Lloyd is a low-end RB2 while this lasts.
  • Arizona: Jeremiyah Love (ankle) is officially questionable — limited all week but "trending well" (LaFleur), expected to play (Weinfuss). If he goes, expect an even split with Tyler Allgeier — a floor trap in Week 1. Sit.
  • Seattle: Price and Holani split work nearly even in Week 1 — Price leads early downs (5.2 YPC); Holani owns third and fourth downs. Both are committee plays.

Still unconfirmed HOLD
