MU — Micron Technology | Watchlist → Researched

As of: Mon Sep 7, 2026

Status: Not a buy/sell recommendation

Thesis

Micron is an AI-memory tightness story colliding with a classic cycle-peak trap. The shortage/HBM demand looks real in company numbers; the research question is whether peak earnings power is a plateau or a cliff — especially after a vertical move through ~$1,000.

Bull

  • FACT: FQ3 revenue $41.46B; non-GAAP EPS $25.11; GM ~85% non-GAAP; HBM4 high-volume shipments (Micron SEC Ex 99.1 / PR Jun 24, 2026).
  • FACT: FQ4 guide revenue $50.0B ± $1.0B; GM ~86%; non-GAAP EPS $31.00 ± $1.00 (same).
  • INFERENCE: If HBM sold-out status and DRAM tightness persist into CY27, earnings can surprise “cycle peak” skeptics.

Bear

  • INFERENCE: Memory is cyclical; post-peak ASP collapse is the historical trap when capacity catches demand.
  • FACT/price: Closed ~$1,016.59 Sep 4 (+~6%) — rising ≠ still undervalued.
  • INFERENCE: “Cheap” P/E on peak quarterly EPS annualized is exactly how cycle bulls get hurt.

Catalyst + date

FACT: FQ4 earnings call Wed Sep 30, 2026, 2:30 p.m. Mountain (Micron IR).

Near-term: CPI-week tape; DRAM/NAND contract price prints.

What would change our mind

  • Toward bear: miss vs FQ4 guide or soft FY27 pricing commentary.
  • Toward bull: extended sold-out HBM + raising long-term customer agreement backlog.

Labels

Company financials/guidance/call date = FACT. Duration of tightness and multiple sustainability = INFERENCE. Not a buy/sell.

Coming to terms

Coming to terms

  • HBM: High-bandwidth memory — specialized DRAM stacked for AI accelerators.
  • Gross margin (GM): Share of revenue left after direct production costs.
  • ASP: Average selling price — the cycle’s main swing variable.
