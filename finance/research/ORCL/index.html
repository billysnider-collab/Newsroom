Does AI backlog turn into cash — or stay a dilution story after the Q1 print?

The one idea

Oracle’s Q1 showed the AI-infra demand is converting (RPO $664B, IaaS +121%) and cash burn was better than feared — but the company still ran negative FCF and raised equity via ATM. The question is backlog quality and funding path, not “is AI real.”

Bull — why it could work
FACT

Revenue $19.3B (+30%); non-GAAP EPS $1.92; cloud $11.6B (+62%); IaaS +121%; RPO $664B with >$30B AI cloud bookings (Oracle IR 2026-09-10).

FACT

FCF −$5.4B vs ~−$9.6B expected (Reuters/LSEG); ~$11.4B customer prepayments against capex; FY27 non-GAAP EPS guide $8.10.

INFER

Prepay/BYO-hardware mix reduces incremental chip cash need vs prior scare narrative.

Bear — what breaks it
FACT

Still negative FCF; $20B ATM equity sale in Q1; interest expense up; S&P had downgraded earlier on cash-flow concerns (Reuters).

INFER

Dilution + rate-hike week can keep multiple capped even on “good” prints.

INFER

Stargate/labor/power execution risk remains a known overhang.

When it matters

Print was Sep 10 AMC; Fri close $150.28 (−1.74%).

Watch: Post-print drift into/after FOMC; any capacity delivery updates.

What would change our mind
  • Toward bear — Capex guidance up without matching prepay; FCF worsens next print; RPO conversion stalls.
  • Toward bull — Sustained FCF improvement; clear conversion of RPO to revenue without further heavy dilution.
Coming to terms — words that make this stick
RPO
Contracted future revenue not yet recognized.
ATM
At-the-market equity issuance — selling stock into the market over time.
FCF
Operating cash flow minus capex.

FACT = company/official. INFER = judgment. Source: ORCL-2026-09-14.md · Not a buy/sell recommendation.
