KR — The Kroger Co. | Watchlist → Researched

As of: Mon Sep 7, 2026

Status: Not a buy/sell recommendation

Thesis

Kroger is a consumer/inflation optics name into the same Friday as CPI — not a “cheap defensive” slogan. The live question is identical-sales and margin quality vs Walmart price competition, with energy-driven food/cost pressure as a second-order input.

Bull

  • FACT: FY2026 adj EPS guide $5.10–$5.30; FIFO OP $5.0–$5.2B; identical sales ex-fuel guide 1.0–2.0% (company guidance trail).
  • INFERENCE: If comps hold and margins defend, a low-beta grocer with buybacks/retail media can outperform into inflation noise.
  • FACT: Q2 call Fri Sep 11, 2026 8:00 a.m. ET (Kroger IR Aug 14).

Bear

  • INFERENCE / secondary: Street downside watches on possible guide cut amid Walmart price investment — secondary, not company.
  • INFERENCE: Failed Albertsons deal left scale gap vs Walmart/Costco; thin grocery margins + commodity inflation squeeze if pass-through fails.
  • INFERENCE: Cheap vs PT can fail if comps decelerate — cheap ≠ undervalued.

Catalyst + date

FACT: Q2 FY2026 earnings conference call Friday, Sep 11, 2026, 8:00 a.m. ET; webcast ir.kroger.com; Q2 ends Aug 15, 2026 (Kroger IR).

Same morning as CPI Aug print — cross-read food-at-home vs company comps.

What would change our mind

  • Toward bear: guide cut + margin miss.
  • Toward bull: comp acceleration + margin stability / raise.

Labels
