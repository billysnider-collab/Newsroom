ADBE — Adobe | Watchlist → Researched

As of: Mon Sep 7, 2026

Status: Not a buy/sell recommendation

Thesis

Adobe is a same-week, opposite failure-mode to Oracle: not “does AI backlog exist?” but “does Creative software still monetize under AI substitution — with a new CEO?” The multiple already looks like growth is gone; Thursday’s ARR quality decides whether that’s priced or premature.

Bull

  • FACT: Board named Anil Chakravarthy president & CEO effective Dec 1, 2026; Shantanu Narayen → executive chair (Adobe Newsroom Sep 3).
  • FACT: Closed $266.51 on Sep 4 (−6.73%); trailing P/E ~15.2x / forward ~10.3x on aggregator prints (stockanalysis) — FACT those are secondary multiples, not a valuation conclusion.
  • INFERENCE: If net-new Digital Media ARR and Firefly/AI attach stabilize, a Hold-consensus name can re-rate without a hero narrative.

Bear

  • FACT: Hard selloff into succession week (Sep 4 tape).
  • INFERENCE / secondary: Street split — Hold dominant; some Underweight framing around AI substitution of Creative Cloud (sell-side wraps — secondary until call).
  • INFERENCE: Soft net-new ARR or guide cut keeps the multiple compressed; cheap ≠ undervalued if the franchise is being substituted.

Catalyst + date

FACT: Q3 FY2026 earnings Thu Sep 10, 2026 (Adobe Newsroom / IR). CEO effective Dec 1, 2026.

Watch: net-new ARR, AI/Firefly monetization, guide, any Chakravarthy appearance as incoming CEO.

What would change our mind

  • Toward bear: soft net-new ARR or cut guidance.
  • Toward bull: clear AI ARR acceleration + stable Creative retention metrics.

Labels

Succession names/dates and earnings date = FACT (Adobe IR). Competitive AI outcome = INFERENCE. Buy/sell = not offered.

Coming to terms

Coming to terms

  • ARR: Annual recurring revenue — subscription dollars that renew/expand.
  • Forward P/E: Price ÷ expected next-year earnings — cheap can still be a value trap.
  • Executive chair: Board leadership after handing off day-to-day CEO duties.
