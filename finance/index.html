Prediction · posted Fri Sept 11, resolves Fri Sept 25

Who owns the bottleneck — the 2-week test

What we know CONFIRMED

  • The hook: August CPI printed "computer software and accessories" up 25.4% year over year — the largest ever recorded (BLS). It looks like software is getting expensive fast.
  • The catch: that number doesn't say what actually got more expensive. The Fed's own researchers note this index isn't quality-adjusted, covers a tiny slice of spending (0.031% weight), and the surge is concentrated in a few months after decades of falling software prices. Don't trade the headline — follow the chain.
  • First order: if AI demand is real, the obvious winners are Nvidia, Microsoft, AMD. That's the trade everyone already sees.
  • Second order: AI needs data moving between chips (Astera Labs, Marvell), optical connections (Coherent, Lumentum), memory (Micron), and cooling for racks that run enormously hot (Vertiv).
  • Third order: the real bottleneck is electricity and physical infrastructure — Eaton, GE Vernova, Amphenol. They don't sell "AI"; they sell what AI physically requires.
  • The prediction: over the next two weeks, the bottleneck basket (equal-weighted ALAB, MRVL, COHR, LITE, MU, VRT, ETN, GEV, APH) outperforms the first-order basket (equal-weighted NVDA, MSFT, AMD), measured from the Sept 11 close to the Sept 25 close. Bottleneck basket ahead = HIT; first-order basket ahead = MISS.
  • This is a thesis test, not investment advice.
