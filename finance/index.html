Coming to terms

Learn the words — then the card makes sense.

NFP (nonfarm payrolls)
The government’s monthly count of how many jobs the U.S. economy added or lost outside farming. Bigger-than-expected = labor looks stronger.
Fed hike odds
What traders in Fed funds futures imply about the chance the Federal Reserve raises its policy interest rate at the next meeting (here ~58% for September).
Hormuz premium
Extra price baked into oil because the Strait of Hormuz shipping lane looks riskier (attacks, lower traffic) — not just “oil went up.”
CPI / PPI
Consumer / Producer Price Indexes — official inflation prints. CPI is what households pay; PPI is what producers receive. This week’s reports can swing Fed-path bets.
ISM Services PMI
A monthly survey of service-business managers. Above 50 ≈ expanding; below 50 ≈ contracting. “Prices paid” tracks how expensive their inputs feel.
RPO (remaining performance obligations)
Contracted future revenue a company like Oracle has on the books — backlog — which still has to convert into cash.
Blackout
The quiet period before a Fed meeting when officials stop giving public guidance, so markets lean harder on data.
ARR
Annual recurring revenue — subscription-style revenue run-rate (Adobe watch). Business-quality check, not a stock tip.
FOMC
The Fed’s policy committee — the meeting that sets the rate decision and the statement markets parse.
