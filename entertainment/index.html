Practical Magic 2 conjures a softer-than-expected $30M — still No. 1

What we know CONFIRMED

  • Deadline's early Saturday numbers point to a ~$30M opening — below the $40–50M industry expectation (BoxOffice Pro); the studio's own $30M target is the low end it barely holds (JoBlo, Deadline).
  • Still enough for No. 1, ending Spider-Man: Brand New Day's six-week run. The 1998 original did $46.7M domestic total — the sequel lands about two-thirds of that in three days.

TV · Mon Sep 14

Widow's Bay sets the record; The Pitt repeats; Rhys makes history

What we know CONFIRMED

  • Widow's Bay took Outstanding Comedy Series and finished with 14 awards — the most by a comedy in a single year, beating The Studio's 13 in 2025 (per EW, People).
  • Matthew Rhys won twice in one night — Lead Actor in a Comedy (Widow's Bay) and Lead Actor in a Limited Series (The Beast in Me) — the first performer to win two lead-acting Emmys in the same year; he now has lead wins in drama, comedy, and limited (per People, Screen Radar).
  • Jean Smart won Lead Actress in a Comedy for Hacks' final season — her fifth win, the first woman to win lead acting in every season of her show (per iHeart).
  • The Pitt repeated as Outstanding Drama Series (7 awards, incl. Noah Wyle for Lead Actor in a Drama); DTF St. Louis took Limited/Anthology (7); Pluribus took 6, incl. Rhea Seehorn as Lead Drama Actress. Supporting acting: Allison Janney (The Diplomat), Tom Pelphrey (Task) (per Reuters, EW).
  • The Late Show With Stephen Colbert won Outstanding Variety Series. Michael J. Fox received the Bob Hope Humanitarian Award to two standing ovations; Taylor Swift cameoed in an SVU sketch (per People).
  • In Memoriam paid tribute to Dolly Parton, Catherine O'Hara, Tim Curry, and the late Rob Reiner — honored with a posthumous Creative Arts win for The Bear (per EW).
