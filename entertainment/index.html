Prime Video cancels Spider-Noir after one season

In plain English

Prime Video is not making a second season of Spider-Noir, the Nicolas Cage 1930s Marvel show. Critics and Emmy voters liked it; Amazon still walked.

What we know

  • Canceled after one season (announced Sep 4, 2026)
  • Rotten Tomatoes: 92% critics / 90% audience
  • 11 Emmy nominations — most for any Prime Video series in 2026
  • Nielsen: 851 million minutes week 1; 2.6 billion over six weeks in the U.S.
  • Amazon–Sony Marvel-characters deal continues; one other project “nearing a series order”
  • Silk was previously scrapped

What we don’t (HOLD)

  • Exact budget figures circulating elsewhere (e.g. ~$400M) — not in Deadline’s core
  • “Cost vs retention” as Amazon’s motive — industry inference, not a company statement

Cage’s “one season is perfect” comment (Deadline exclusive Sep 6) is a confirmed reaction — not a renewal factor.

Why it matters

A well-reviewed, Emmy-nominated Marvel show still got canceled — proof that scores alone don’t guarantee a second season on streaming.

Sources

Deadline primary · Variety · TVLine (same-day independent corroboration)
