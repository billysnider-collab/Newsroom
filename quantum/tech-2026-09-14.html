Biggest breakthrough

FDA accepted the BLA and granted Priority Review; PDUFA target March 10, 2027; no advisory committee currently planned (Intellia / GlobeNewswire, Sep 8). Phase 3 HAELO (n=80): 87% cut in mean monthly HAE attacks vs placebo (weeks 5–28); 62% attack-free and therapy-free vs 11% placebo. Sponsor safety window: mild/moderate TEAE only in lonvo-z arm; no SAEs through week 28.
    FACT: Priority Review + dated PDUFA.
    REJECT: “approved” / commercial — NOT approved, NOT commercial. Casgevy is ex vivo; lonvo-z would be category-creating if cleared — one 50 mg outpatient infusion (KLKB1 knockout) vs lifelong prophylaxis.

  2. Runner-up — DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash (Sep 10). 552B MoE; KV cache ~1/4 HBM vs prior Flash; MIT open weights; as of 04:00 UTC Sep 14, deepseek-v4-pro routes to Flash at Flash rates. Live cost cut for agent/cache-heavy work.

Coming to terms — words that make this stick
PDUFA
FDA Prescription Drug User Fee Act target date — when the agency aims to decide on a drug application. Lonvo-z: March 10, 2027. A decision date is not an approval.
In vivo CRISPR
Gene edit delivered inside the patient’s body (here: one infusion knocking out KLKB1 in the liver). Ex vivo (Casgevy) removes cells, edits them, and returns them.
MoE
Mixture-of-Experts — a large model that activates only a slice of parameters per token. DeepSeek V4.1-Flash: 552B total, smaller active path + compressed KV cache.
CoWoS
TSMC’s advanced chip-packaging process that still bottlenecks AI accelerators. Lead times >12 months; packaging capacity matters more than another GPU keynote.
QEC
Quantum error correction — fixing fragile qubit errors as a machine runs. The difference between a noisy demo and a computer that keeps its answer. Helix is an architecture result on that path, not a product.
PPA
Power purchase agreement — a long-term contract to buy electricity. Google’s Loviisa deal underwrites an existing nuclear plant’s life extension, not fusion.

Quantum — Helix architecture, not product

Brother-test: Error correction is the difference between a noisy demo and a computer that keeps its answer while it works. This week’s best result is a better blueprint — not a product you buy to break crypto or design drugs.

1) Quantinuum C4-Helix on Helios (98 physical qubits)

Paper arXiv:2609.03194 (submitted Sep 2; coverage ~Sep 8–9). Code [[20,2,6]] C4-Helix — ~3.5× lower spatial overhead vs rotated surface code of similar distance (company/analyst framing).

  • FACT Logical memory: 4.6×10⁻⁵ errors per logical qubit per QEC cycle
  • FACT Logical two-qubit Clifford: 2.8×10⁻⁴ error — ~4.3× better than unencoded physical (~1.2×10⁻³)
  • FACT Inter-code GHZ fidelity lower bound 99.925%
  • Architecture: protected memory + Clifford compute + interface between codes
  • Missing: sustained non-Clifford/magic-state without postselection; streaming real-time decoding (Frontier decoder used offline here)

HOLD early-FT architecture validation on commercial hardware.

REJECT “useful quantum computer / crypto timeline moved.” Technical milestone ≠ commercial usefulness.
