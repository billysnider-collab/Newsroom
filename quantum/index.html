Visible mistakes beat invisible ones

Most quantum errors hide. These qubits usually “lose the photon,” which the machine can see. D-Wave + Yale built a fast two-qubit gate that still mostly makes visible mistakes instead of invisible ones — which makes fixing mistakes much easier later.

What changed

First hardware-validated ~500 ns CZ on dual-rail cavity erasure qubits

Superconducting dual-rail cavity erasure qubits that keep the erasure-over-Pauli hierarchy during two-qubit ops — not just at rest.
