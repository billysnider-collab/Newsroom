Arctic · the announcement

The “Infinite Life” deal — Trump announces permanent US control of Greenland’s security

What we know CONFIRMED

  • Trump announced Friday on Truth Social: an “Infinite Life Agreement” giving the US “permanent control over security, and all other needs” in Greenland — at no cost to taxpayers, with an immediate “large military presence.”
  • Danish PM Frederiksen confirmed the deal and welcomed it as respecting Danish sovereignty and Greenlandic self-determination.
  • Signing expected during UNGA week (Sept 21); Danish and Greenlandic parliamentary ratification still required.
  • A State Dept official: China, Russia, and other non-NATO countries barred from bases or sensitive investments without US approval.

Still unconfirmed HOLD

  • The deal text — and how far it differs from the existing 1951 Defense of Greenland Treaty, which already gave the US broad military access.
  • Whether the UNGA-week signing grants anything genuinely new, or is new paint on the 1951 treaty.

Sanctions · the signature

Graham signed — the 100% tariff weapon goes live

What we know CONFIRMED

  • Signed Friday: H.R. 5334, the Lindsey O. Graham Sanctioning Russia and Iran Act of 2026 (White House statement, per Reuters) — named for the late Sen. Graham, who died in July.
  • What it does: authorizes up to 100% tariffs on major buyers of Russian oil (China, India), mandates blocking sanctions within 30 days, and extends Iran sanctions.
  • Passed the House 262-159 (Sept 16) and the Senate 86-11 (August).
  • The authority is discretionary: Trump decides whether and whom to hit. Trump–Xi meet at the White House Thursday (Sept 24) — five days out.

Still unconfirmed HOLD

  • Whether Trump fires the tariff weapon at anyone, and at what rate.
  • Whether Treasury designates shadow-fleet tankers before UNGA week.
