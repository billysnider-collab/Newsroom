FOMC · today

The Fed decides at 2 PM — Warsh vs. the White House that hired him

What we know CONFIRMED

  • Day 2 of the FOMC; decision 2 PM ET, Chair Kevin Warsh's press conference 2:30 ET — the first hike since 2023 after five meetings on hold.
  • ~91–93% odds of a 25bp hike to 3.75–4.00% (per Barron's/CME FedWatch). Markets are pricing roughly four more hikes between now and next June (per Brookings' Robin Brooks via Reuters).
  • Trump picked Warsh expecting rate cuts; Warsh is about to deliver a hike seven weeks before the midterms — into $108 oil, $4.36 gas, and a record $6.23 diesel (per AAA/Barron's Monday data).
  • The house isn't unanimous: Standard Chartered's Davies/Englander openly argue for a hold — "very low cost to waiting."
  • Warsh's Aug 28 signal ("work to do" if data didn't improve); August CPI rose again (per USA Today). The 2:30 presser will be parsed word-by-word — Warsh dislikes forward guidance (per WSJ).
  • The hike is priced; the presser is the event. History says cycles don't stop at one — the only modern one-and-done was 1997 (per Barron's).

Still unconfirmed HOLD

  • The vote itself; whether the dot plot shows more hikes this year; how Warsh handles the Trump-pressure question at the presser. All resolved by ~2:30 PM ET.

Mecca · Tuesday 6:50 PM local

Saudi Arabia says it shot down a Houthi drone headed for Mecca

What we know CONFIRMED

  • Saudi air defenses destroyed a Houthi drone south of Mecca Tuesday at 6:50 PM local, before it entered prohibited airspace (per Reuters, coalition spokesman Turki al-Malki).
  • al-Malki called it the second attempt on the holy city ever — the first since a 2017 ballistic missile (per Reuters).
  • The Houthis deny it — spokesman Yahya Saree says targets were "far removed from the holy places" (per CNN). The 57-nation OIC condemned the attempt.
  • Why it matters: Mecca is the one red line that could unify the Muslim world behind a Saudi war effort and force a US response — Saudi Arabia already asked Washington for direct military help against the Houthis (per Reuters, Sept 12).
  • Whether the drone was actually aimed at Mecca or passing nearby is the difference between an incident and an accelerant.

Still unconfirmed HOLD

  • The drone's actual intended target — dueling claims, unresolved. Whether Washington answers the Saudi request for direct military help.

Russia · Ukraine

The "energy truce" that lasted hours

  • Trump's Monday claim that both sides "agreed not to hit each other's energy targets" was downgraded by both capitals within hours: Zelenskyy called it a "strong US proposal for a mutual halt" needing allied guarantees and long-term reliability; Peskov called it a "very good idea" but conditioned it on sanctions removal and safe tanker shipping (per Reuters, CNN).
  • Overnight: Zelenskyy says a Russian FPV drone struck an intercity minibus near Nikopol — 5 killed, 7 injured (per Zelenskyy, 4:25 AM ET).
  • Kyiv took ~80 drones overnight, 63 downed, a warehouse fire in Sviatoshynskyi, 1 injured (per Al Arabiya English citing Klitschko).
  • Tuesday: Russia hit Kyiv petrol stations; Ukraine struck a Russian refinery (per Reuters, CNN). The stakes are physical: Ukraine's refinery campaign forced Russia to ban diesel exports (the world's #2 diesel exporter), and diesel is at records (per Reuters/AP).

Still unconfirmed HOLD

  • Whether any enforceable mechanism is even on the table. Damage details at the struck refineries.

DOJ · Tuesday

Five Russian operatives charged in an assassination network on US soil

What we know CONFIRMED

  • DOJ unsealed an indictment Tuesday charging five Russian intelligence operatives with conspiracy to finance terrorism and murder-for-hire: ex-colonel Yuri Khrameev (63), his son Kirill (27, alleged FSB officer), Cubans Oemis Romagoza Durruthy (35) and Yaidel Delgado Suarez (35), and Venezuelan Angel Eduardo Castro (22). All five are at large (per DOJ, Reuters).
  • This summer, a Brooklyn recruit was offered $1,000–1,500 to surveil a Russian dissident believed in the DC area and $40,000 to "eliminate" or "disappear" him — the recruit declined the killing (per DOJ/Reuters/WaEx).
  • Another US recruit was offered $25,000 last year for a killing in Lithuania (per Reuters); the network also commissioned terrorism against civilian and military infrastructure in pro-Ukraine European countries (per DOJ).
  • AG Todd Blanche: "stop America's adversaries from unleashing violence and terror on American soil." Peskov declined comment pending "credible evidence" (per Reuters).

Still unconfirmed HOLD

  • The dissident's identity (DOJ withheld it); whether any plot advanced beyond surveillance. The next indictment tranche — networks like this rarely get fully rolled up in one filing.

Carry-forwards

China's exit rules take effect; Canada Day 2; VTB waits

What we know CONFIRMED

  • China's exit-and-entry rules took effect Tuesday — citizens violating export controls or deemed tech-security threats can be barred from leaving (6-month to 3-year bans); foreigners face 1–5 year entry bans for false visa statements (per Reuters, WSJ). The first time export-control violations are explicitly linked to exit bans. Read as leverage-setting nine days before the unconfirmed Sept 24 Trump–Xi meeting.

  • Treasury's VTB designation stands; no foreign bank has publicly exited yet — Bessent vowed sanctions on foreign banks "every week" (per Law.com).
  • Canada's expanded 50% Section 338 list is on Day 2; the Sept 29 import bans (dairy, alcohol, motorcycles) are next. Carney: "We have everything we need to pivot and prosper" (per The Times) — Canada is drifting toward the EU as US talks stay collapsed.

  • Enforcement scope of the exit rules; the first foreign bank to exit VTB; any Sept 29 carve-outs.
