RIYADH’S SCHOOLS GO REMOTE — “A SMALL-SCALE REHEARSAL”

What we know CONFIRMED

  • NEW — Saudi authorities ordered Riyadh schools to remote learning for a week on Sunday, a day after the Saturday-dawn wave (The Media Line); parents received Madrasati-platform notices; the education ministry gave no public explanation. The capital’s classrooms closing is the most visible domestic signal of the war’s second front. (MEDIUM — single origin, attributed)
  • The Saturday-dawn wave, clarified: the coalition destroyed 2 Houthi drones headed toward the Riyadh area and intercepted 2 ballistic missiles aimed at Khamis Mushait (coalition statement, late Saturday). Civil-defense alerts went out for Khamis Mushait and Abha — not Riyadh. No casualties or damage confirmed. (Reuters/Arab News via Particle)
  • NEW — on the record: Houthi Political Bureau member Hezam al-Assad called the attack “merely a small-scale rehearsal for a full-scale escalation.”
  • NEW — the Sunday tape (Reuters): Saudi TASI closed -0.3% on the Houthi claims; ACWA Power -2.8%; Aramco recovered from early losses to close +1.3%; Qatar -1.1%; Egypt’s EGX30 -0.2%. US embassies across the Middle East warned travelers to stay vigilant (flight cancellations, airspace closures).
  • The coalition spokesman is now a named source: Maj Gen Turki Al-Malki (SPA). Friday’s Makkah pact chiefs condemned attacks targeting Mecca and civilian infrastructure; France is deploying soldiers, radar and air defenses to Yanbu (Macron).
  • The desk’s read: the schools decision is Riyadh pricing in a sustained campaign, not a one-off wave — and the “rehearsal” quote says the Houthis want it read exactly that way.

Still unconfirmed HOLD

  • The interceptor-shortage claim — AP’s “regional officials” say Saudi asked France, Britain, Pakistan and Egypt for air-defense support (single anonymous sourcing, seen-only).
  • China privately urging Iran to restrain the Houthis after a Saudi appeal (Reuters, via Iranian sources — single-source, seen-only).
  • Whether the Mecca pact gets formally triggered; whether French or British air-defense help materializes.

Vatican · Lourdes

TWO HOURS WITH SEVEN SURVIVORS

What we know CONFIRMED

  • NEW — Reuters: Pope Leo met seven French abuse survivors privately at Lourdes on Sunday — two hours, each survivor met individually, with proposals heard for improving the Church’s safeguarding. The Vatican: he “thanked them for their painful testimony and reaffirmed his commitment to ensuring that the harm they experienced is not repeated for others.”
  • The survivors afterward: the meeting was emotional; they felt listened to and were impressed by his compassion and knowledge of their individual cases — but they want the words accompanied by action. Jérôme Guillement: the Church in France “still has a tremendous amount left to do.”
  • Earlier Sunday: about 150,000 at the Lourdes field Mass, where Leo said the Church must have the courage to face its past — “heal the deep wounds, infidelities and painful errors.” He also met France’s bishops behind closed doors on safeguarding. Context: a 2021 report found about 330,000 French children abused by clergy and lay leaders over seven decades.
  • Monday: the Metz leg, then the Monday-evening press conference on the plane (Le Monde) — the next print on what, if anything, follows the words.
  • The desk’s read: the first US pope’s first major abuse-reckoning moment — the survivors’ “but” is the story. Compassion registered; action pending.

Still unconfirmed HOLD

  • What concrete safeguarding steps follow the meeting.
  • Monday’s plane press-conference answers.
