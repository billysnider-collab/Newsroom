Russia-Ukraine · the largest ever

Ukraine’s “largest ever” attack on Moscow — 1,000+ drones, the refinery hit on election night

What we know CONFIRMED

  • Overnight into Sunday: Ukraine fired over 1,000 drones at Russia, hundreds toward Moscow — the largest drone attack on the Russian capital ever, per Moscow Mayor Sobyanin (AP/NPR, 5:16 AM EDT Sunday).
  • Damage: the Moscow Oil Refinery at Kapotnya — which supplies roughly half the capital’s fuel, including its airports — and a residential building; Moscow airports hit with delays and cancellations.
  • 2 dead (a 74-year-old man and a 44-year-old woman), 20 wounded in the wider Moscow region, per Gov. Vorobiev.
  • Zelensky confirmed: “our long-range response made itself felt” — domestically made Flamingo and Pelican missiles used on oil and logistics facilities.
  • It landed on the final day of Russia’s wartime parliamentary elections.
  • Russia answered on Kyiv overnight: drone strikes on the Kyiv region killed 4, including 3 children (a mother and her two 3-year-olds in Hlevakha, plus a girl who died in hospital); 31 sites damaged across five districts (Reuters, Sunday).
  • Zelensky: Russia launched 2,000+ drones, 1,700 aerial bombs and 19 missiles this week alone.
  • State Dept approved a $2.7B air-defense arms sale to Ukraine Saturday — missiles, laser-guided systems, counter-drone tech; funded by European contributions plus Biden-era aid; proceeds unless Congress objects within 30 days (NY Post).

Still unconfirmed HOLD

  • The true penetration rate — Moscow claims near-total interception (1,110 per Russia’s MOD; 450 approaching Moscow per Sobyanin).
  • The damage extent at the refinery.
  • Whether this marks a new tempo or a one-off election-day message.

Iran · the big decision

Tuesday decides it — Trump’s “annihilate” interview and the six Gulf leaders

What we know CONFIRMED

  • Trump told Axios (Barak Ravid interview, published Thursday) he faces a “big decision” on whether to renew large-scale attacks aimed at annihilating the Iranian regime: “I have a big decision coming up. Do I want to go in and annihilate them [the Iranian regime] or do I not? It’s a big decision. Anything could happen with me.”
  • He wants the Gulf leaders’ read first — meeting Tuesday in NYC, on the UNGA sidelines, with Saudi Arabia, the UAE, Qatar, Bahrain, Kuwait, and Oman. Axios: resuming major combat operations would need those governments’ buy-in.
  • Trump declined to say whether he decides before or after the Nov 3 midterms; “I may” see Netanyahu in New York.
  • Iran knocked down two US MQ-1 Predator drones in recent days (CBS, via NY Post).
  • Russia and China vetoed a UNSC bid to extend the expert panel monitoring Iran sanctions.
  • Hundreds of thousands marched in Tehran Friday — the largest show of government support since the Feb 28 strikes (AP).

Still unconfirmed HOLD

  • What “annihilate” means operationally — the US and Israel already tried decapitation strikes in February/March.
  • Whether the Gulf leaders buy in; whether Trump decides this week at all.
