FZ1073 — MORNING

THE BEN GURION PLAN — PLANNED BEFORE THE HIRE

What we know CONFIRMED

  • Two independent reporting lines landed in the last 12 hours. Channel 12 reported Hamam al-Hammami told investigators he intended to fly FZ1073 toward Israel as if nothing was wrong, obtain the clearances to approach Ben Gurion Airport, and crash the aircraft into a building or another plane — per information from the Saudi and Emirati investigations (via Fox News). Reuters separately reported Monday that a source briefed on the UAE investigation said the Omani co-pilot planned to crash the flight into Ben Gurion Airport, and that two Israeli officials with knowledge of the investigation said al-Hammami told questioning he planned the attack BEFORE flydubai hired him. (HIGH as reported claims)
  • The UAE Attorney-General stated Saturday that the co-pilot used an emergency crash axe in the cockpit in an attempted “terrorist act.” (HIGH)
  • Reuters adds the flight plunged 16,000 feet in about 30 seconds before being leveled out, per an aviation instructor who recreated it in a simulator. (HIGH as reported)
  • In Australia, the Victorian Joint Counter Terrorism Team (federal police, Victorian state police, ASIO) is investigating the co-pilot's links to the country — two sources briefed on the investigation said he studied at a university in Melbourne; RMIT confirmed he was enrolled about a decade ago but never completed an engineering qualification (per Reuters). (HIGH on the probe; MEDIUM on the RMIT detail)
  • Netanyahu praised a heroic bystander for preventing “another 9/11.” (MEDIUM-HIGH, via Fox News)
  • Captain Smit Machchhar is in good condition; flydubai's Tel Aviv suspension stands. (HIGH)
  • The desk's read: this is the story's phase change — from a cockpit incident to a planned mass-casualty attack on an airport, conceived before the attacker was even hired. The hiring chain is now the accountability story, and the Australia/RMIT thread is a second investigative front.
  • EVENING: Reuters Monday adds the pre-hire detail in the co-pilot's own words as relayed by two Israeli officials — he told UAE interrogators he planned the attack BEFORE flydubai hired him, and applied to the airline specifically because it flies to Tel Aviv. A source briefed on the UAE probe confirmed the Ben Gurion plan (obtain clearances, then ram a building or parked aircraft). UAE and Israeli authorities declined comment; flydubai did not respond. (HIGH as reported claims)
  • EVENING: Israeli investigators have not questioned him directly — he is in UAE custody. No formal charges announced. The contingency plan to crash into a US military base in Jordan, the pre-employment planning quote, and the 'visited Ben Gurion on recon' detail are all single-source Hebrew media (Channel 12/13 via anonymous sources) — not independently confirmed. (HOLD)

Still unconfirmed HOLD

  • Whether formal charges have been announced; whether UAE or Saudi authorities confirm the Channel 12 Ben Gurion detail; the full RMIT timeline; motive beyond what questioning produced; whether the Jordan-base contingency or the recon-visit claim corroborates.
