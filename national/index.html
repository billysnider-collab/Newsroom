THE AMENDED PLAN — TUESDAY/WEDNESDAY IS THE BINARY

What we know CONFIRMED

  • Reuters (Sept 28, an official briefed on the negotiations): Qatari mediators met Iranian FM Araghchi in New York Monday to work an amended version of the seven-day proposal Iran tabled at UNGA; the Qataris carry it to Washington, with the US reply due Tuesday or Wednesday. (HIGH)
  • Iran's ISNA confirmed Araghchi met the mediators Monday morning NY time; he flew home within hours — 'Whenever the Qataris have a response, they know how to get it to us.' (HIGH)
  • The channel now runs through Qatari AND Pakistani mediators and has taken 'a more serious form' since Iran submitted its proposal (Araghchi's words to Iranian state media). (HIGH)
  • The plan's shape: end hostilities in Iran and Lebanon, unfreeze Iranian assets, end oil sanctions and the US port blockade — then reopen Hormuz, then resume nuclear talks. Trump rejected it Saturday as 'unacceptable'; Sunday he told Axios he expects talks 'this week.'

  • Both sides are privately pricing escalation after the midterms: Iranian officials are privately pessimistic about any deal before November 3 (Bloomberg); Trump's 'I am always thinking about it' on strikes says the same from the other side. (MEDIUM)
  • The desk's read: watch for the reply, not the rhetoric — a dated next round means the paper has legs; silence past Wednesday means the rejection stood.

Still unconfirmed HOLD

  • Who represents the US side with the mediators; what the amendments change; whether the reply lands Tuesday or Wednesday.
  • Whether 'no nuclear, no deal' is the final US position or the opening one.
