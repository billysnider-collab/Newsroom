SCOTUS · Monday

Three sentences, no rescue — the USPS voting plan dies

What we know CONFIRMED

  • The Supreme Court rejected the administration's USPS mail-in voting rule Monday in an unsigned three-sentence order (CNN, Reuters).
  • The majority found the administration "unlikely to succeed on the merits" — the strongest language available at this stage (CNN, Reuters).
  • Only Justices Alito and Thomas dissented; Justice Kavanaugh concurred on timing grounds (CNN, Reuters).
  • Tuesday, Trump turned on his own appointees on Truth Social: the justices who opposed him are "not the people I interviewed" — "merely a shell of their original selves" — claiming the Court is "bullied and cajoled by the Radical Left" (per Reuters, USA Today). Gorsuch, Coney Barrett, and Kavanaugh — all three of his first-term picks — ruled against him.
  • At a White House press briefing Tuesday, AG Todd Blanche left the door open for 2028: "President Trump's efforts to make sure that we have free and fair elections are not going to stop just in November or after or thereafter" (per CNN).
  • Election officials say the ruling makes them confident the fight won't disrupt November's midterms (per Reuters); Brookings (2025) found mail-voting fraud in about 4 cases per 10 million ballots cast (per TNND/Fox49).

Still unconfirmed HOLD

  • Any narrower rule or executive workaround before November — the only door Blanche named is 2028.

Treasury · Monday

VTB designated under Iran sanctions authorities

What we know CONFIRMED

  • Treasury on Monday designated Russia's VTB Bank — the country's second-largest lender — under Iran sanctions authorities (EO 13902) for helping Tehran evade sanctions, exactly the Monday action Bessent previewed Friday (Reuters, WSJ, CNN).
  • Bessent calls VTB "among the most comprehensively sanctioned financial institutions in the world" (Reuters). WSJ: VTB provides clearing accounts to the Central Bank of Iran and sanctioned Iranian banks for rials/ruble exchange, moved billions in frozen Iranian assets, and has 1,000+ branches globally with a notable presence in India.
  • VTB was already under US sanctions since February 2022 — the marginal bite is the secondary-sanctions warning: Treasury warned foreign banks still dealing with VTB face "even more sanctions risk."
  • The sharpest financial escalation since Operation Economic Outcast (nearly 60 entities, individuals, and vessels hit last month).

Still unconfirmed HOLD

  • Which foreign banks actually cut VTB ties — no named bank has exited yet; that is the tell.
  • Whether this meaningfully degrades Iran's oil-revenue plumbing or is mostly deterrence-by-warning.

Trade · Tuesday 12:01 AM ET

Canada tariffs in effect — no pause, stacking on 232

What we know CONFIRMED

  • The revised 50% product list took effect at 12:01 AM ET today with no White House walk-back; the Federal Register proclamation confirms the duties stack on top of existing Section 232 tariffs.
  • Ottawa's trade minister Dominic LeBlanc says Canada is "assessing" the measures (via Le Monde wire); PM Carney: "We have everything we need to pivot and prosper."

Still unconfirmed HOLD

Energy · Day 5

Wright: oil flowing "within days" — first named-official restart signal

What we know CONFIRMED

  • The East-West pipeline is still shut on Day 5 — 4–5M bpd offline. US Energy Secretary Chris Wright told CNBC Tuesday that oil should be flowing through it "within days" (per Reuters) — the first named-official restart signal; previous hopes were unnamed sources only.
  • WSJ's explainer: the system is two parallel lines with separate pumping infrastructure — one line could resume after safety checks while repairs continue on the other. Roughly 4M bpd was flowing pre-shutdown (~4% of global supply). Full repair: 6–8 weeks.
  • Brent settled at $108.75 (+2.9%), WTI at $105.83 (+4.4%) Tuesday — the highest closes since May 19 (WSJ). US retail diesel hit a fresh record ~$6.27/gal (AAA); NYMEX diesel futures +6.1% to a record $5.262/gal.
  • Reuters: Saudi Arabia has suspended oil loadings at the port of Yanbu. Kpler: the damage could cut Yanbu exports by 2.5–2.7M b/d. Libya shut in production at three sites after guards closed a valve (WSJ).
  • The Houthis seized Perim Island in the Bab al-Mandeb over the weekend after taking the port of Mokha (WSJ) — tightening the second chokepoint. CNBC satellite images (Sept 14) show fire damage and blackened ground at a pumping station.
  • UKMTO Warning 135-26 stands: a vessel struck by an unknown projectile in the Strait of Hormuz overnight (~5:14 AM ET) — no damage; attacker, flag, and cargo unknown (UKMTO).

Still unconfirmed HOLD

  • Attribution for the overnight projectile strike — HOLD.
  • The El Gaia dispute: Iran says a naval mine; CENTCOM says an Iranian drone. Crew: 13 of 14 Indian nationals rescued, one missing (ANI via The Tribune) — HOLD.
  • The observed restart — Wright's "within days" is a signal, not a flow. Reports that the 400k b/d YASREF refinery in Yanbu was struck (single report via OPIS/Morningstar) — HOLD.

Russia · Ukraine

Trump announced a truce. Tuesday's strikes confirmed it on both sides.

What we know CONFIRMED

  • Trump said Monday that Ukraine and Russia agreed to halt attacks on each other's energy infrastructure (Reuters, CNN).
  • Tuesday's answer: Russia launched ~200 drones at Kyiv and other cities, hitting petrol stations in the capital — at least 2 killed (Reuters). Poland commenced preventive military aviation operations to secure its airspace (per Poland's Armed Forces).
  • Ukraine's General Staff says it hit the Syzran refinery in the Samara region — fire and damage to a crude-oil distillation unit and storage tanks. The Samara governor confirmed an industrial facility was hit and 40+ drones downed, without naming it.
  • In Taganrog, an Ozon warehouse and ag-company warehouses were struck (per Governor Slyusar); Ukraine's General Staff says it hit the Atlant Aero drone plant there — and a drone preparation/launch site in the Oryol region, plus targets in the Black Sea (per Zelenskyy).
  • Tuesday, Kremlin spokesman Peskov called the proposal "a very good idea" — then demanded safe commercial shipping and sanctions relief: "Only then will the world be adequately supplied" (Reuters). Zelenskyy said Kyiv would support an energy ceasefire only with credible guarantees that Moscow stops too.
  • IEA context: a Russian refinery was hit on average every three days in the first eight months of 2026.

Still unconfirmed HOLD

  • Full damage extent at Syzran, Taganrog, and Oryol.
  • Whether any back-channel terms exist behind the "proposal."

NATO · Lithuania

First drone ever shot down over Lithuanian territory

What we know CONFIRMED

  • President Gitanas Nauseda announced on X that NATO fighter jets destroyed a drone that entered Lithuanian airspace shortly after midnight Tuesday — the first time a drone has been shot down over Lithuanian territory.
  • Lithuania's Defense Ministry thanked Italian pilots — the Italian Air Force unit based at Siauliai for NATO's Baltic air-policing mission.
  • National Crisis Management Centre: the drone crossed from Belarus, moved through Lentvaris/Trakai heading west, and came down near Pratkūnai in Kaunas county.
  • Per Lithuanian outlet Lrytas: the interception happened near the Kruonis Pumped Storage Plant — the Baltics' only one, holding 94% of Lithuania's emergency energy reserves. Last month the Defense Ministry had flagged Kruonis as a top potential target.
  • Foreign Minister Budrys: NATO stands "vigilant, committed, and ready." Moscow had not commented as of Tuesday reporting.
  • Distinct from Sunday's incident: a drone sighting that closed Vilnius airport then turned out to be a flock of birds (Reuters).

Still unconfirmed HOLD

  • The drone's origin, type, and intent — the centre confirmed the Belarus crossing but not precise origin; attribution is the whole story.
  • NATO's official statement; any Russian response.

Rates · the Fed week

The 10-year printed 5% — then closed just under

What we know CONFIRMED

  • The 10-year hit 5.04% intraday Tuesday — the first print above 5% since 2007 — then closed at 4.995% (WSJ). The 30-year stood at 5.37%, the 20-year at 5.40% (TradingView/Benzinga).
  • A global bond selloff: German 10Y at 3.57% (highest since 2009), JGB 10Y at 3.036% (30-year high).
  • Bessent told lawmakers the deficit is one factor in the 10-year's yield, but the Treasury does "not set equilibrium price for yield levels" (TradingView/Benzinga).
  • The Fed's two-day meeting began Tuesday; decision Wednesday ~2:00 PM ET, Warsh presser ~2:30 PM ET. CME FedWatch via WSJ: 94% odds of a 25bp hike to 3.75–4.00% (up from 59% a week ago).
  • Reuters' poll: 85% of 101 economists expect the hike; 53% expect at least one more by end-March. Barron's via CME: 79% odds of a half-point or more of increases through year-end.
  • Warsh's dilemma, per the wires: Trump picked him expecting cuts, and a hike lands seven weeks before the midterms.

Still unconfirmed HOLD

  • The statement language — one hike vs the start of a cycle is the trade.
  • Whether 5% on the 10-year becomes resistance.
