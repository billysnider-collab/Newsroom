Space · Starship Flight 14

FIRST ORBITAL STARSHIP — 26 REVENUE SATELLITES UP, MISSION CUT SHORT

What we know CONFIRMED

  • NEW MIDDAY — Starship lifted off from SpaceX’s Starbase, Texas, Monday morning (about 7:46 AM CT, Reuters) — the 14th Starship test and the first to reach orbit; all 13 previous flights since April 2023 were suborbital. (HIGH)
  • First revenue flight: Starship deployed 26 Starlink V3 satellites — the first operational payload release (Reuters; Florida Today live coverage). (HIGH)
  • SpaceX cut the mission short: the planned ~10-hour, six-orbit voyage became ~3 hours in orbit after an upper-stage engine was lost during the flight; the ship was deorbited into a pre-cleared Pacific zone (Barron’s; Florida Today). (HIGH)
  • Starship splashed down in the Pacific about 12:17 PM ET Monday, ending the first orbital mission (Florida Today live). (HIGH)
  • Musk declared success on X; NASA Administrator Jared Isaacman congratulated SpaceX on the launch (Florida Today). (MEDIUM — single outlet)
  • The desk’s read: the orbital benchmark Musk once promised for 2022 finally cleared — with a paying payload aboard. Starship is now a working rocket, not a test article.

Still unconfirmed HOLD

  • The cause of the upper-stage engine loss; whether the ~10-hour profile is re-attempted on the next flight.
  • Starlink V3 service timing and the orbital AI-data-center (Starmind) timeline.

“WE MAY DO IT” — THE DIESEL BAN IS BACK ON THE TABLE

What we know CONFIRMED

  • At the Presidents Cup Sunday, Trump told Fox News he’s “looking at it very seriously — we may do it” on a diesel export ban (The Times, Bloomberg). That reopens what Sen. Cruz’s Saturday White House assurance had closed. (HIGH)
  • US diesel sits at a record: AAA $6.4531; the $6.5276 record from Sept 22 stands; regular gasoline $4.4768.
  • Goldman math: a ban cuts US diesel ~$0.25/gal per week but raises European wholesale ~$3/gal and US gasoline ~$0.30/gal per week once storage fills. (MEDIUM)
  • Energy Secretary Wright is working with refiners on voluntary export curbs as the alternative; oil CEOs, AFPM, API and the Chamber sent a letter last week urging Trump to abandon the ban. (HIGH)
  • The UK angle: diesel hit a record 199.18p Monday; ministers privately warn of shortages; Britain holds 42 days of reserves; about a third of its diesel imports come from the US. (The Times)
  • VLO, MPC and PSX edged up overnight; diesel futures climbed. Trump blamed the spike on the Russia–Ukraine war and said he told Zelenskyy to “take it easy on the refineries.”
  • The desk’s read: the Saturday assurance vs the Sunday threat is the administration’s indecision on camera. The refiner stocks will price the decision before the market does.

Still unconfirmed HOLD

  • Ban, voluntary curbs, or neither; the timing of any announcement.

JET DRONES HIT KYIV’S ACADEMY OF SCIENCES — AT LEAST 7 KILLED

What we know CONFIRMED

  • NEW EVENING — Russia’s daytime drone wave hit Kyiv’s National Academy of Sciences around 1:30 PM local Monday: a direct jet-drone hit, one person killed, six injured; the building burned (DSNS, via AP/Reuters). Ukraine’s education minister: Russia is trying to intimidate children, parents, educators, and scientists. (HIGH)
  • The Monday toll, per Ukrainian officials (AP/Reuters): at least 7 killed and 80+ wounded — Kyiv 4 dead / 19 wounded (including 3 children); Kyiv Oblast 3 dead / 8 wounded; Dnipro business center 3 dead / 12 wounded; a Kharkiv apartment block 43 wounded (11 children); Synelnykove 1 killed.   • Zelensky: Russia is now hitting Kyiv with faster, high-flying jet drones that Ukraine intercepts at just over half — down sharply from the propeller-drone interception rate. (HIGH on the claim)
  • Russia claimed strikes on data centers, gas stations, and cargo vessels bound for Odesa/Chornomorsk/Izmail — Reuters could not independently verify. (HOLD)
  • Carry-forward: Trump on CNN Saturday declined to confirm Zelensky’s “final decision” on Patriot-manufacturing licences: “Make a deal. I want him to settle with Putin.” The ~Oct 5 checkpoint stands.
  • The desk’s read (provisional pro-Ukraine framing): Kyiv is absorbing the retaliation wave while the licence fight stays unresolved in Washington. Watch which moves first — the intercept rate or the licence text.

Still unconfirmed HOLD

  • The final casualty count; Russia’s cargo-vessel and data-center claims (unverified).
  • Whether any Patriot licence text exists; the UAE meeting’s date and agenda; what Dmitriev is offering.

Trade · Trump–Xi

THE $60B DETAILS LANDED — LISTS REAL, TEETH PENDING

What we know CONFIRMED

  • Late Sunday the White House announced the “30-for-30” framework: $30B each way in tariff cuts on non-sensitive goods (CNN); the truce extended two months to Jan 10. (HIGH)
  • China published its list: 1,619 US items — corn, wheat, sorghum, meat, dairy, veg oils, timber, medical devices, coal — soybeans NOT included, a blow to US farmers. (HIGH)

  • The desk’s read: grade it a B+ — the lists are real, the truce is real, the teeth are pending. The soybean exclusion is the farm-belt tell.

Still unconfirmed HOLD
