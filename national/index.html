Russia · Ukraine · overnight

Yaroslavl, confirmed — Russia's own governor says the refinery was hit

What we know CONFIRMED

  • Reuters: four industry sources say the Slavneft-YANOS refinery halted crude processing Thursday after the overnight Ukrainian drone attack.
  • Regional governor Mikhail Yevrayev confirmed on Telegram: "65 drones were downed... The oil refinery sustained damage, and fires are being actively extinguished."
  • The damaged unit is AVT-3 — roughly 40% of the plant's capacity; AVT-4 (33%) was already under repair from the Aug 28 strike. Yaroslavl fuel was not offered on Thursday's domestic commodity exchange.
  • The 300,000-bpd plant is ~Russia's fourth-largest (Slavneft, a Rosneft/Gazprom Neft JV).
  • Ukraine says the strike was a four-agency joint operation deep in central Russia (~250 km from Moscow) — a Ukrainian claim, not independently verified.

Still unconfirmed HOLD

  • Any Kremlin-level statement (none found).
  • The exact drone mix; the repair timeline at Yaroslavl.
  • Whether Trump signs the Graham bill and whether he uses the secondary-tariff authority (Meeks: no guarantee).

Energy · Day 8

No October crude for Europe, but a restart “within days”?

What we know CONFIRMED

  • Bloomberg via Reuters, Friday: Aramco told at least two European refiners they will receive NO crude in October — October deliveries cancelled. Orlen (Poland) bought North Sea grades to replace Saudi imports. Reuters could not independently verify; Aramco did not comment.
  • Meanwhile Bloomberg, citing a person familiar: Aramco aims to partially restart the East-West Pipeline within days and return to full capacity within six weeks.
  • Those two Aramco-source lines sit awkwardly together — and both sit against the Reuters satellite-analysis assessment: THREE pumping stations damaged, 5-6 weeks to repair (three industry sources); partial pumping could resume sooner. The market is watching the contradiction, not picking a side.
  • The workaround is now a confirmed trade flow: ~60M bbl of Ras Tanura crude committed for Sept/Oct loading via ship-to-ship transfer off Oman’s Sohar port — Chinese and South Korean refiners the primary buyers, India and Japan also in (per Reuters, traders).   • Brent closed ~$103.25 Friday (-1.5%) — the third straight down day from Tuesday’s ~$108.77 high; WTI fell roughly 2%, into the high $90s. The market has decided the workaround is the physical story that counts.

Sanctions · the signature

Trump signs the Graham act into law — Russian energy in the crosshairs

What we know CONFIRMED

  • Signed Friday, Sept 18: H.R. 5334, the Lindsey O. Graham Sanctioning Russia and Iran Act of 2026 (White House statement, per Reuters).
  • What it does: targets Russian energy, defense, and the shadow fleet; authorizes up to 100% tariffs on the top five importers of Russian oil and gas — India in the crosshairs. Five-year sunset; prevents a lapse in Iran sanctions authority.
  • The House passed it 262-159 on Wednesday; Trump sat on it for two days after a White House official told WSJ he planned to sign.
  • China had already condemned the bill’s long-arm jurisdiction (per Reuters).

Still unconfirmed HOLD

  • No tariff designations or implementation timeline yet.
  • Whether Trump uses the secondary-tariff authority (Meeks: no guarantee).
