Iran · Day 209

A PHASED PATH OUT OF THE WAR — HORMUZ FOR THE BLOCKADE

What we know CONFIRMED

  • Reuters, Friday (Nakhoul/Hafezi/Irish): US and Iranian negotiators in New York are exploring a phased path out of the nearly seven-month war — Tehran reopens the Strait of Hormuz, Washington lifts its economic blockade, Tehran potentially gains access to frozen assets.
  • A senior Iranian official, on the UNGA sidelines: "One way forward would be to solve the crisis in stages. The first would be to end the blockade and reopen Hormuz."
  • The obstacle: neither side wants to be the first to surrender its leverage — and an earlier understanding collapsed in July, so durability, not just shape, is the negotiation.
  • Trump has said he thinks a deal could come after the Nov 3 midterms; a senior European official said the Iranians "have a very long list of demands."
  • Pezeshkian, to Fox News Thursday: "It’s America that must choose whether it wants to end this or not" — putting the war-ending decision on Trump’s desk, in American media, in English.
  • The market heard it: oil gave back ~2% Friday morning (Brent ~$104.85–105.45, WTI ~$92.40–92.85) — before the tankers have moved at all.

Still unconfirmed HOLD

  • Whether the US backs off sanctions in stages or as one move; whether Hormuz "reopening" means escorted corridors or full normal traffic.
  • Attribution on this week’s missile fire at Saudi; anything verifiable on Salalah or the cargo-seizure bill.
  • Any named US official echoing the phased framework — the next verification point.

TIGRAY — THE NEW FRONT

What we know CONFIRMED

  • War broke out this week between Ethiopia’s federal military and Tigray forces plus allies — the most serious escalation since the 2020–2022 war.
  • Tigrayan forces seized three local airports and launched attacks in Afar and Amhara.
  • NetBlocks confirmed a subnational internet disruption in northern Ethiopia Friday.
  • Panic buying in Tigray — residents told Reuters shelves are being emptied.
