Houthis · the escalation ladder

Saturday’s twin operation — “sensitive sites” in Riyadh, Aramco’s Yanbu hub

What we know CONFIRMED

  • The Houthis claimed a “large number” of ballistic and cruise missiles plus drones against “sensitive sites” in Riyadh and an Aramco facility in Yanbu (military spokesman Yahya Sarea, on Al-Masirah).
  • Saudi coalition spokesman Turki al-Maliki said air defenses intercepted a ballistic missile toward Riyadh and foiled other attempts on Baish, Taif, Farasan and Yanbu.
  • AFP-verified video showed flames at an Aramco fuel depot near Riyadh’s King Khalid International Airport — something got through.
  • Saudi civil defense issued its first Riyadh aerial-threat alert of the escalation (Reuters journalist heard booms; all-clear Saturday morning).
  • Trump told Fox’s Trey Yingst (Sunday) the Houthis have agreed not to attack US troops (per Barron’s) — even as they hit Saudi targets. The split is the story: the Houthis’ war is now explicitly Riyadh’s problem, not Washington’s.

Still unconfirmed HOLD

  • The real damage at Yanbu (Saudi coalition says foiled; Houthis say successful).
  • Whether Riyadh’s read is escalation-pressure for Tuesday or a genuine war-widening.
  • What “agreed not to attack US troops” actually covers and how Trump verified it.

Iran · Tuesday decides

Trump opened the diplomacy door; Iran’s conditions are with the mediators

What we know CONFIRMED

  • Trump said Sunday he is open to meeting Iranian President Masoud Pezeshkian on the UNGA sidelines this week (per Barron’s live coverage, citing Trump remarks Sunday).
  • Iran’s security chief Mohsen Rezaei told Al Jazeera (Saturday) that Iran has conveyed its conditions to mediators for re-engaging negotiations; contents undisclosed.
  • Tuesday in NYC: Trump meets six Gulf leaders (Saudi Arabia, the UAE, Qatar, Bahrain, Kuwait, Oman) — the “big decision” read-out from the Axios interview.
  • The Graham act (signed Friday): up to 100% tariffs on top Russian-oil buyers, discretionary; the 30-day blocking-sanctions clock runs; Treasury shadow-fleet designations are the early tell.
  • Iran’s central military command warned Sunday it has information the US and regional partners are preparing a new attack; any attack triggers sustained strikes on US bases and interests, and countries that assist would be treated as parties to the conflict (Tasnim via Reuters/Fox/CNN).
  • UNGA choreography: Trump takes the rostrum Tuesday; Pezeshkian addresses Wednesday (Reuters/Mehr via Fox); the Iranian delegation’s visas were approved with strict State Dept movement limits in Manhattan.
  • The Salalah meeting is off: a Monday GCC+Iran+Iraq regional meeting in Salalah, Oman was postponed, per Omani FM Badr al-Busaidi (Qatari FM confirmed). Separate from Tuesday’s NYC meeting.
  • State Dept warned Saturday the conflict “has the potential to escalate rapidly”; the US Embassy in Saudi Arabia restricted personnel travel to Taif and Yanbu without authorization (CNN).

Still unconfirmed HOLD

  • The contents of Iran’s conditions.
  • Whether the Pezeshkian meeting actually happens; whether the Gulf buy in or veto again.
  • What “annihilate” would mean operationally.

Russia-Ukraine · the refinery war

Ukraine took the refinery war to Moscow’s fuel supply — retaliation watch is live

What we know CONFIRMED

  • Ukrainian General Staff says the Moscow refinery’s AVT-6 primary crude distillation unit, the integrated crude processing unit and the isomerization unit were hit, with large fires recorded (per rigzone, Sunday).
  • The refinery is Gazprom Neft’s, ~25 km from the Kremlin, ~245,000 bpd capacity — roughly 40% of Moscow’s fuel market and 70% of the region’s petrol/aviation demand (investinglive).
  • Zelensky confirmed first confirmed combat use of the Pelican ballistic missile.
  • Russia claims 1,110 drones downed nationwide (MOD) / 450 approaching Moscow (Sobyanin); casualties: 2 dead, 20 injured in the Moscow region per Gov. Vorobyov (some outlets report 3 dead — contested); 400 evacuated in Ramenskoye, including 70 children (ABC).
  • Russia also struck Kherson — 2 dead including an election official (Reuters).
  • United Russia won the wartime Duma election — VCIOM exit poll 49.4%, early official partial results ~57.5% (Reuters/Le Monde); voting also held in occupied Donetsk, Luhansk, Kherson and Zaporizhzhia (denounced by Kyiv).
  • State Dept approved a $2.7B air-defense arms sale to Ukraine Saturday — missiles, laser-guided systems, counter-drone tech; European-funded; proceeds unless Congress objects within 30 days (NY Post).

Still unconfirmed HOLD

  • The real refinery outage duration — Gazprom Neft hasn’t commented.
  • The true interception rate.
  • Moscow’s retaliation target and timing — Russia’s pattern is a 24–72 hour reply.

Hormuz · four strikes in six days

Monday’s UKMTO strike — the traffic says the strait is still working

What we know CONFIRMED

  • Monday 07:12 CT: UKMTO reported a tanker struck by a projectile in the Strait of Hormuz — two crew with minor injuries, the vessel continuing to its next port (Reuters via UKMTO advisory). Single Tier-A source, no vessel ID yet, no second source; attribution HOLD.
  • The fourth tanker incident since Sept 16 (three Sept 16–18: IRGC TREND claim; two projectile strikes — attribution HOLD).
  • CENTCOM chief Admiral Brad Cooper posted Saturday that oil/cargo/LNG volumes through Hormuz over the past two weeks were the highest in six months; Saudi exports recovered above 4M bpd in September vs 2.4M in August (Kpler, via Reuters).
  • VLCC rates last read $870,947/day (DeepDraft, Sept 19) — the insurance market still pricing risk.

Still unconfirmed HOLD

  • Attribution on the projectile hits (all four).
  • The TREND detention — the IRGC’s claimed strike-and-detention of the Togo-flagged product tanker is unconfirmed (AP could not confirm).
  • Confirmation of any Sohar STS loading.
