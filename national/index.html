Iran · Tuesday is the day

The offer is real enough to move oil — today decides whether it survives contact

What we know CONFIRMED

  • A senior Iranian official told Reuters Tuesday morning that Iran could reopen the Strait of Hormuz to Gulf shipping within days if the US lifts its blockade — calling UNGA a "golden opportunity" for diplomacy. Iran sent its latest proposal via mediators on September 16; the delegation has full authority to revive talks. No direct Trump–Pezeshkian meeting is planned.
  • Secretary of State Marco Rubio told NBC’s "Today" the US is open to speaking with the Iranians at UNGA, "especially if it has the prospects of leading to something positive."
  • Trump’s Tuesday schedule (NYC): a morning UNGA address, the trilateral Greenland signing at 10:30 AM ET, a meeting with six Gulf leaders (Saudi Arabia, the UAE, Qatar, Bahrain, Kuwait, Oman), and bilaterals with Ukraine’s Zelensky, Venezuela’s interim leader Delcy Rodríguez, the UK’s Andy Burnham and Japan’s Sanae Takaichi (iHeart/CNN).
  • Pezeshkian leads Iran’s delegation — arrived Tuesday; addresses the assembly (Tasnim).
  • The counter-signal: Iran’s military command (Khatam al-Anbia HQ, via Tasnim) says its intelligence suggests a new large-scale US strike is being prepared and warns of "painful" retaliation across the region; countries backing a strike would be treated as parties to the conflict. Trump’s Sunday line to Fox: the options are "wiping Iran out" or letting its economy "rot" absent a deal.
  • The precedent both sides remember: no face-to-face Iran–US meetings are known since July, when an interim deal signed by both presidents collapsed weeks later.
  • Brent broke below $98 on the Reuters exclusive — intraday low $97.76 (−2.6%), the fifth straight down session. The market believes the diplomacy branch has a pulse.

Still unconfirmed HOLD

  • What "lifts its blockade" concretely means — and whether the Gulf leaders buy in or veto.
  • The Axios-via-secondhand claim that Iran fired missiles at commercial vessels Monday — single low-quality source, unverified.
