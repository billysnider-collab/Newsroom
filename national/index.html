Iran · the annihilate speech

Two Iran tracks in one day: the threat and the hallway

What we know CONFIRMED

  • Trump’s 38-minute UNGA address Tuesday framed the Iran choice as a deal that lets Tehran rebuild “maybe one of the greatest in the Middle East” — vs “annihilate the Islamic Republic… drive them into hell with no chance of survival.” He predicted a deal “right after the election” (Nov 3 midterms): “I’m not running. I did that already and won in a landslide.” (Times, NY Post, TBS)
  • The walkout: Iran’s delegation walked out about ten minutes in; one lower-level diplomat stayed. Cuba’s delegation walked out later — Trump called Cuba a “failed state” that “will fall.” Pezeshkian and Araghchi were not in the hall; Pezeshkian addresses the assembly today. (Telegraph Nigeria, NY Post)
  • The hallway: at his Zelensky bilateral, Trump said US officials held a “very good” three-hour meeting with Iranian officials Tuesday — Witkoff and Kushner on the sidelines (NY Post); the WSJ calls it the first high-level US–Iran meeting since June.
  • The expanded table: Trump’s Mideast meeting ran beyond the GCC — 12 countries (GCC 6 plus Iraq, Jordan, Turkey, Syria, Lebanon, Egypt, per White House pool/ToI via Newsmax). Trump claimed “a lot of momentum” for an Iran deal and warned the US could strike the Pickaxe Mountain nuclear facility if activity there rises. CNN: the Gulf came in with two fears — more war, less US protection.
  • New detail (NY Post): Trump said he called off a major Iran attack last month after Saudi Arabia, the UAE and Qatar told him a Hormuz-reopening deal was within reach. The deal never materialized.
  • The market’s verdict on which track is real: Brent dipped to $98.44 overnight, then bounced back above $100 Wednesday morning — investors “unconvinced by reports of progress” (WSJ).

Greenland · signed

Greenland signed — now read the fine print

What we know CONFIRMED

  • Signed Tuesday at UNGA (Trump, Frederiksen, Nielsen). Per reporting on the text: it amends the 1951 US–Danish defense treaty, creates two new US “Defense Areas” (Narsarsuaq, Mestersvig) alongside an expanded Pituffik, bars non-NATO countries from military installations or influence over “particularly sensitive” sectors, allows a Golden Dome deployment (content undecided, per Frederiksen) — and has no end date. (Reuters, USA Today)
  • It does not take effect until Danish and Greenlandic parliaments complete their procedures. The location, scope and size of the new sites are left to future talks, escalating to ministerial level if no agreement within 90 days; construction contracts go to Greenlandic firms “to the maximum extent possible.”
  • The quotes: Frederiksen — “a deal that can last forever”; Nielsen — “not only an agreement about us, it is an agreement with us” and “win-win-win.”

Still unconfirmed HOLD

  • The full published text; what “permanent control over security” costs in practice; the ratification timeline in Copenhagen and Nuuk.

Kyiv · the truce died in a news cycle

Hours after the truce pitch, Russia hit Kyiv’s petrol stations

What we know CONFIRMED

  • Hours after Zelensky told Trump he was ready for “any format” of energy ceasefire, Russia slammed jet drones into two Kyiv petrol stations (Holosiivskyi, Darnytskyi), a business center, warehouses and railway infrastructure Wednesday morning — 2 killed, 22 injured per the updated Reuters wire via city authorities (revised up from the initial 1/7).
  • Ukrnafta: the sixth such facility hit in recent weeks. The EU ambassador posted burning buildings from the delegation’s windows.
  • Zelensky also asked Trump for more Patriots for the expected winter campaign against Ukraine’s power grid — no agreements. Wednesday, the Kremlin said the grounds for future peace talks “are still not in place” and no high-level meetings are planned.
