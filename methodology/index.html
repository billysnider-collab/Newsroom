How we grade — the rules behind every CONFIRMED and HOLD on this site.

The loop

Claim → Evidence → Grade → What remains unknown

  • Every factual claim on this site passes through the same loop. The grade is printed next to the claim, and what we still don't know is printed right below it.
  • Grades are not decorations. They are counted mechanically from the page markup — the totals on the homepage come from the claims themselves, not from an editor's summary.

What CONFIRMED means CONFIRMED

  • Evidence: supported by sufficient primary or credible evidence — an official statement, a data release, a wire report from the scene, or direct documentation.
  • Source quality: primary sources first. Named officials and on-the-record material outrank anonymous sourcing; established wires outrank aggregators.
  • Corroboration: material claims are confirmed by at least two independent credible reports, or one primary source of record.
  • Attribution: company and government claims are labeled as claims — "Iran said," "CENTCOM reports" — not stated as unattributed fact.

What HOLD means HOLD

  • HOLD does not mean false. It means the evidence isn't sufficient to call it confirmed — yet.
  • Single-source reports, anonymous sourcing without corroboration, numbers that are still moving, meetings that are reported but unconfirmed by the named parties.
  • HOLDs stay visible on the page. Nothing gets quietly dropped when it's inconvenient.

What would change a grade

  • HOLD → CONFIRMED: a second independent report, an official confirmation, or the release of the underlying data.
  • CONFIRMED → CORRECTED: new evidence showing the original claim was partially wrong — the correction is logged publicly.
  • Any grade → CONTRADICTED: credible evidence against the claim. Contradictions are printed, not buried.

The full scale

  • CONFIRMED — cleared the bar above. CORRECTED — was wrong, now fixed, logged. CONTRADICTED — evidence weighs against it. UNVERIFIED — cannot be checked with available sources. MISLEADING — technically true but framed to deceive.
  • On story pages we use CONFIRMED / HOLD as working shorthand. The full scale applies everywhere the desk publishes.
