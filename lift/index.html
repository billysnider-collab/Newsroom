WONDER · keeper 1 of 3

A 1949 humpback song, misfiled as “fish noises”

What happened

Woods Hole archivists digitized Gray Audograph discs and found a humpback whale song recorded March 7, 1949 near Bermuda — believed the oldest preserved whale recording, about 20 years before Roger Payne’s discovery. Scientists didn’t know what they heard; they saved the discs anyway.

Why it lifts

Curiosity left in a drawer can still open a quieter ocean for us to hear.

Source: Woods Hole (WHOI) · Feb 10, 2026

ANIMAL · keeper 2 of 3

A thousand “extinct” snails flown home

What happened

Campbell’s keeled glass snails — presumed extinct for decades, then rediscovered on Norfolk Island in 2020 — were captive-bred by Taronga Zoo Sydney and partners. About 1,000 snails were released into Norfolk Island National Park (1,300+ total). Wild-born juveniles already show up at the site.

Why it lifts

Even the smallest, slowest lives can stage a comeback when someone bothers to notice.

Source: Taronga Zoo · ~July 2026

LUMINOUS HUMAN · keeper 3 of 3

A teen who redesigned the crosswalk for his grandparents

What happened

Jay Jung, 18, built SmartBlink — computer vision that reads pedestrian walking speed and lengthens the walk phase (about 90–95% accuracy on wheelchairs and crutches). Inspired by grandparents in Daegu missing the light. Regeneron ISEF 2026 1st place, Behavioral and Social Sciences.

Why it lifts

Small daily indignities, noticed with love, become infrastructure that restores dignity.

Source: Nice News · ~July 2026
